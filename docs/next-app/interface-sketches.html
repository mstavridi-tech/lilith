Interface sketches · first round

Three ways the app could look

These are rough sketches built from what you've told me: sleek, sexy, modern and minimal, still fun, easy to read, and never so busy that she closes it and forgets it. Each direction shows the same four screens with the same content, so you're comparing the look and feel, not the features.

Scroll each row sideways to see all four screens. Pick the one that feels most like you, or tell me which parts to mix.

  • Three tabs and one buttonToday, Receipts, You. The middle button is the daily check-in.
  • Today shows three thingsHow she is and why, her alter ego, and what's coming up.
  • Words before numbersA plain sentence first. Raw signals underneath, never an invented score.
  • Only her mix showsThe Focus slider appears because ADHD is in her mix. Nothing she doesn't have ever shows.
Direction A

Near-black, ivory type and one champagne accent. Closest to the ring apps your friends already use, with a fashion-house serif for the headlines. Quiet, expensive and calm at night. The risk: dark apps can feel heavy for long reading.

My recommendation

Porcelain by day, Noir by night, with Phase colour as the accent

Porcelain reads best for every age and makes the doctor report feel trustworthy. Noir is its dark mode, so the app follows her phone's setting. From Phase colour, keep one idea: a small phase-coloured detail on Today, like the ring and the eyebrow, rather than painting the whole screen. That keeps it discreet in public and still fun.
