# Greek names for LILITH's grown-up sister

Research date: October 20, 2026. Brief: a Greek-rooted name for a women's health app (ages 22 to 55) for complicated, overlapping conditions and meds (PMDD, ADHD, PCOS, GLP-1s, perimenopause). Three layers: Body (facts), Self (confidence alter ego), Sky (moon rhythm). The brand should feel like a fashion house or a fragrance, and a 55-year-old should be happy to say it to her endocrinologist.

Already excluded by the brief: Athena, Aphrodite, Gaia, Nyx, Selene, Eos, Hera, Artemis, Theia, Psyche, Kairos, Soma, Metis, Hesper/Vesper, plus generic wellness words (bloom, flo, glow, thrive, aura, luna).

**Method and limits.** App Store checks used `site:apps.apple.com <name>` web searches. Trademark checks used web searches that surface Justia, USPTO aggregators (gleanmark, trademarkdir) and EUIPO mentions. Direct Justia, EUIPO and RDAP/WHOIS lookups were blocked from this sandbox, so every **domain is UNVERIFIED** and **no exact-name EUIPO search was possible**. A clean result here means "nothing surfaced", not "clear to file". Before committing, run a real clearance search (USPTO, EUIPO eSearch, WIPO Global Brand Database, UAE Ministry of Economy) in Classes 9, 42 and 44, ideally through a trademark attorney. Arabic notes come from general knowledge. Have a native Gulf Arabic speaker sanity-check the final pick.

## 2. Shortlist of 12, with conflict checks

Columns: App Store / brands / trademarks / domain (UNVERIFIED) / meanings in other languages / verdict.

### MELETE
- **App Store:** no exact match (nearest: "Mele", a Syriac language app).
- **Brands:** the Open University's Melete scholarship scheme ("Ideas into Action"). No health or beauty use found.
- **Trademarks:** no MELETE record surfaced in US searches. Nearest is MELETEX, an Indian Class 5 pharma application filed in 2026. No EUIPO hit surfaced.
- **Domain:** UNVERIFIED. The .com is likely held as a dictionary/mythology word; plan on a modifier (getmelete, melete.health).
- **Languages:** Greek is positive (study, care). English: people may read it as "mel-EET" and hear "delete". Arabic: nothing found. Spanish: nothing found.
- **Verdict:** cleanest. The only real risk is pronunciation.

### PHASIS
- **App Store:** no exact match. But the "Phase" lane is crowded with cycle and menopause apps: "Phase: Menopause & HRT", "Phase: Period & Cycle Tracker", "Cycle Syncing Tracker - Phase", "Phase: Cycle Synced Workouts", plus the HerPhase and Phases startups.
- **Trademarks:** no exact PHASIS record surfaced. Near marks are PHASICS (French optics, Class 9), PHASIC and PHASEX. Their goods are unrelated.
- **Domain:** UNVERIFIED.
- **Languages:** Greek is positive (the slang φάση is cool and current). English hears "phases", and it sits near "aphasia". Arabic: nothing problematic found, though the "fas-" opening should be checked by a native speaker. Spanish: nothing found.
- **Verdict:** strong meaning. The risk is confusion with all the "Phase" apps, both for trademark examiners and in App Store search.

### GALENE
- **App Store:** no exact match (GalenBook is a Spanish medical library).
- **Brands:** Galène is open-source video-conferencing server software. Galénic is a French luxury skincare brand founded by Pierre Fabre, now owned by Yatsen: close in the beauty space but a different word.
- **Trademarks:** no GALENE record surfaced. GALEN (a supplements application, 2024) and GALEN HEALTHCARE exist.
- **Domain:** UNVERIFIED.
- **Languages:** modern Greek γαλήνη (serenity) is lovely and is also a woman's name. English speakers may say "GAL-een". Arabic and Spanish: nothing found.
- **Verdict:** low to medium risk. Doctor-friendly. Weaker on the data and receipts story.

### MESTRA
- **App Store:** no exact match. Nearby are MAESTRA (a MAS Medical Staffing caregiver app), Maistra and Maestral.
- **Brands:** Mestra is a Spanish dental products and equipment maker exporting to 50+ countries (Class 10, healthcare-adjacent). Separately, MESTRA AG holds an unrelated instruments mark.
- **Domain:** UNVERIFIED. mestra.com is probably the dental company's.
- **Languages:** Spanish and Portuguese echo maestra/mestra ("teacher, master"), which is positive. English half-hears "mistress", which is sexy for the brand and risky for the doctor. Arabic: nothing found.
- **Verdict:** medium risk. The myth is dark (her father sold her over and over, and she escaped each time by changing form), so tell it as escape and sovereignty.

### THYMOS
- **App Store:** no exact match. "Thymos Research" publishes a small Turkish medical app (via a third-party tracker).
- **Brands:** Thymia, a London mental-health AI startup with the same root, in a nearby space. Thymosin (TB-500) is a grey-market biohacker peptide. The Thymes is a bath and body brand.
- **Trademarks:** no THYMOS record surfaced. THUMOS USA is in security services.
- **Domain:** UNVERIFIED.
- **Languages:** modern Greek θυμός means ANGER, so Greek speakers hear "rage". Spanish speakers pronounce it "TEE-mos", which sounds like *timos* ("scams, rip-offs"). English: "thigh-mos" or "thy".
- **Verdict:** medium. The story is great (an endocrine gland plus courage), but two languages work against it.

### PRONOIA
- **App Store:** no match.
- **Brands:** Pronoia Peaks LLC (supplements, 2022 filings, Class 5).
- **Domain:** UNVERIFIED.
- **Languages:** in modern Greek πρόνοια also means state welfare or benefits (κοινωνική πρόνοια), which feels bureaucratic to Greek ears. In English it is a witty play on "paranoia", and some may hear a mental-health joke.
- **Verdict:** low trademark risk, medium meaning risk.

### MOIRA (cut on conflict)
- **Trademarks:** the MOIRA word mark is REGISTERED (Reg. 5813778, Class 3 cosmetics) to Moira Cosmetics, an LA makeup brand sold widely. They filed a new application in July 2026.
- **App Store:** an astrology and manifestation app called "Moira" was resubmitted to Apple in 2025 to 2026 according to a developer's post, and there is a "Moira Voices" reflective-prompts iOS app.
- **Verdict:** HIGH risk. It's a shame, because the story (fate, plus "degree" in Greek) is beautiful.

### HORA (cut on conflict)
- **Brands:** "Hora Health" is a hormonal-health companion startup (symptoms, cycle patterns, endocrine disruptors), the same category as ours. There's also a "Hora" sport and wellness app on the App Store, and Spanish *hora* is just "hour".
- **Verdict:** HIGH risk.

### HEXIS (cut on conflict)
- **App Store:** "Hexis" (Applied Behaviour Systems) is a live athlete-fueling app with wearable sync, the same adjacent space.
- **Trademarks:** several HEXIS marks are live (Class 9, 42, 17). Hexis S.A. is a French film and vinyl maker.
- **Verdict:** HIGH risk.

### METRA (cut on conflict)
- In the US, Metra is the Chicago commuter rail, with many apps under that name. METRA is also a Columbus, GA transit system.
- Spanish: Venezuelan *metra* means a marble, and it is also slang for a machine gun.
- **Verdict:** HIGH confusion risk, despite the great womb/measure pun.

### PHYSIS (cut on meaning)
- Physis is a Chaski Corp gym-management app (Android). PHYSIS was a 1980 health-club mark (status unknown). PHYSISENS is a pending wellness-monitoring application.
- In medicine, "physis" is the bone growth plate, so an endocrinologist will literally think of pediatric growth.
- **Verdict:** medium to high risk.

### PALMOS (cut on meaning)
- No App Store or trademark hit. But it reads as "Palm OS". Spanish *palmar* is colloquial for "to die" (*palmó* = "he kicked the bucket").
- **Verdict:** medium. It also has the weakest lux feel.

## 3. Top 6, ranked

### 1. MELETE
- **Say it:** MEL-eh-tee (rhymes with "Betty", three beats). Never "mel-EET".
- **The story:** "In ancient Greek, Melete was one of the three original Muses, the Muse of practice and attention. In modern Greek, *meleti* is the word for a study. So the app is a study of you, and the practice of becoming her."
- **Fit:** Body (the study, the doctor-ready report), Self (practice, the alter ego), Sky (a Muse). It is quiet and literary, and works as a fragrance name. It is also a word a doctor respects.
- **Risk: LOW.** No App Store, USPTO or brand conflict surfaced. The only issue is pronunciation. Fix it in onboarding and the first ad ("Melete. Like *melody*, then *tee*").

### 2. PHASIS
- **Say it:** FAY-sis.
- **The story:** "In ancient Greek, *phasis* means the phase of the moon, the moment a star appears, AND a statement, a bold assertion. In Greek slang today, *fasi* means the vibe."
- **Fit:** All three layers in one word (cycle phase, showing up as herself, the moon). It is the richest meaning on the list.
- **Risk: MEDIUM.** No exact trademark found, but there are at least four "Phase" women's-cycle and menopause apps. Expect App Store search confusion and possible examiner objections in Class 9. Clearance is a must.

### 3. GALENE
- **Say it:** guh-LEE-nee.
- **The story:** "Galene was the Nereid of the calm sea, and in Greek today *galini* means serenity. It also echoes Galen, the father of medicine."
- **Fit:** Calm, adult, doctor-safe and very fragrance-like. Weaker on the data and receipts side.
- **Risk: LOW to MEDIUM.** It is close to Galénic (French luxury skincare) and to Galène (video software). No exact trademark found.

### 4. MESTRA
- **Say it:** MESS-tra.
- **The story:** "Mestra was the woman in Greek myth who could change her shape at will. Every time someone tried to sell her, she became someone else and walked away. She's the alter ego."
- **Fit:** The best match for the confidence alter ego. Sexy, and echoes *maestra* (master) in Spanish. Weaker on body and sky.
- **Risk: MEDIUM.** A Spanish dental supplier called Mestra exists (healthcare-adjacent). The "mistress" echo cuts both ways, and the myth needs careful framing.

### 5. THYMOS
- **Say it:** THEE-mos (Greek) or THY-mos (English). Pick one and own it.
- **The story:** "To Plato, *thymos* was the fire in your chest: spirit, courage, self-respect. It also gave its name to the thymus, one of your endocrine glands."
- **Fit:** A great endocrinologist moment, plus a confidence layer.
- **Risk: MEDIUM.** Modern Greek reads it as "anger". Spanish speakers hear *timos* ("scams"). It also sits near Thymia (a mental-health startup) and the thymosin peptide.

### 6. PRONOIA
- **Say it:** pro-NOY-ah.
- **The story:** "*Pronoia* is Greek for foresight. In English it has become the opposite of paranoia: the suspicion that the universe is quietly conspiring in your favor."
- **Fit:** The wittiest name on the list, with forward-looking pattern-finding as the product truth.
- **Risk: LOW to MEDIUM.** The only conflict found is a supplements company (Pronoia Peaks). The modern Greek "welfare office" meaning and the closeness to "paranoia" may undercut lux for some.

**Honorable mention:** MOIRA would be top 3 on story alone (fate, your share, a degree of the zodiac), but Moira Cosmetics holds a registered US mark. Only pursue it with an attorney's blessing.

**Wildcard for the LILITH lineage:** APSIS. Black Moon Lilith *is* the moon's apogee, which is an apsis, so the name makes it literally LILITH's sister. Not checked for conflicts; flag it if Maria loves the lineage story.
