## 5. Revenue scenarios, months 1-18 (launch January 2027)

**Shared assumptions:**
- Apple takes 15% and refunds take 3%, both deducted.
- Annual plans are booked as cash when charged.
- The monthly plan cohort churns at a constant rate.
- Annual cohorts renew at month 13 at the stated rate.
- Download-to-paid is measured at D35. It is anchored to the RC H&F median of 2.9% and top quartile of 6.2%.
- Month 1 includes a launch spike. Downloads are mostly organic (content, PR, waitlist), with light Apple Ads tests.
- The model script is at /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-lilith/5aac3a38-f16a-5379-9dee-4f61ad05ac63/scratchpad/model.py

| Assumption | Conservative | Base | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downloads in month 1, then month 2 | 3,000 → 1,500 | 9,000 → 4,000 | 20,000 → 10,000 |
| Downloads/month by month 18 | 3,300 | 10,000 | 25,000 |
| Total downloads over 18 months | 44,600 | 132,500 | 325,000 |
| Download to paid | 2.0% | 3.5% | 5.5% |
| Price, annual / monthly | $39.99 / $5.99 | $49.99 / $7.99 | $69.99 / $9.99 |
| Share choosing annual | 60% | 65% | 70% |
| Monthly-plan churn | 14%/mo | 11%/mo | 9%/mo |
| Annual renewal (month 13) | 30% | 40% | 50% |

**Notes:**
- **Conservative** does not cover a Dubai cost of living. It is a side-income outcome and signals a pivot or a repositioning.
- **Base** covers costs and a modest salary by year 2. It needs about 4-10K downloads a month, which means a working organic engine: TikTok/IG content in the brand voice, condition communities (PMDD, ADHD and PCOS subreddits and Discords), and press.
- **Optimistic** needs top-quartile conversion and about 20K downloads a month. That is realistic only with a viral loop or a feature placement. Annual proceeds stay under $1M in 2027, so the 15% rate holds. Proceeds would approach the threshold in 2028.
- **Biggest lever:** conversion rate. The hard-paywall data (10.7% vs 2.1% D35) argues for a hard paywall or a 2-week trial on the clinical layer (doctor report, labs, pattern-finding), with a generous free core. Year-1 retention is the same either way (27-28%).
- **Second lever:** price. Moving from $49.99 to $69.99 raises revenue about 40% per payer. Oura, Finch and Welltory show this audience pays $70-99 for credible data insight.
