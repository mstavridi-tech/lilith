# Stardust user survey, October 2026: what it reveals

Source: https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/KLRM5KC (sent to Maria as a Stardust user). Read on Oct 22, 2026 by walking each branch of the first question. Raw text: stardust-survey-oct-2026-raw.txt.

## Structure
- Intro promises ~10 minutes, anonymous answers, and a free month of Premium (code THANKSLUV) for finishing.
- Q1 (required): "What's your primary cycle tracking goal?" decides the branch.
- Only three goals get a real survey: **Trying to conceive, Perimenopause/menopause, Partner mode**. Every other goal (pregnancy, postpartum, avoiding pregnancy, next period, getting to know my cycle, none) goes straight to: "Right now, we're conducting an investigation into some other journeys in the Stardust universe: Trying to Conceive, Perimenopause, and Partner Mode."

## What Stardust is building next
1. **Trying to conceive (already has a TTC mode):** asks about fertility treatments, diagnoses (PCOS, endometriosis, thyroid, insulin resistance, PMDD, low progesterone), how she tracks ovulation (strips, BBT, wearable temping, Oura, Apple Watch, Inito, Mira), partner involvement and intimacy, and whether she uses AI chatbots for info.
2. **Perimenopause (new):** asks her biggest priority, including "Figuring out if my current treatment is helping" and "Understanding patterns in my mood, energy, and symptoms"; 13 symptoms; competitor trackers (Balance, Health & Her, Stella, Caria, Bearable, Clue, Flo, Natural Cycles, wearables); at-home hormone tests (Inito, Clearblue, Mira, Proov); care providers (Midi, Alloy, Winona, Evernow, Hers, Maven); and a required HRT question.
3. **Partner mode (exists):** existing "spells" and hints (snacks, cuddles, alone time); asks what would make it more useful and "Imagine Stardust as a little helper for your relationship. What would you want it to do?"

## What it means for Mestra
- **Perimenopause is now contested.** Stardust is researching a peri mode that tracks whether treatment (HRT) is helping and patterns in mood and energy. That is close to Mestra's promise for peri users. Do not lead with perimenopause alone.
- **The open lane holds.** No questions about ADHD, stimulants, GLP-1s, multiple medications outside fertility and HRT, or doctor-ready reports. Mestra's core (several conditions and meds at once, read through the cycle, with a report for the doctor) is still unclaimed by Stardust.
- **They are watching wearables and hormone tests** (Oura, Apple Watch, Inito, Mira). Expect integrations. Mestra's Apple Health depth must be good from day one.
- **AI is coming.** "Little helper for your relationship" plus the chatbot question suggest an AI assistant.
- **Copy for Mestra's own survey:** branch by goal, reward completion with a free month, and use their competitor lists as a ready-made research list.

## Note on method
Only Q1 was answered in each branch; every later question was skipped. The TTC and perimenopause branches stop at required questions, which were not answered. The partner branch has no required questions, so it ran to the end, and its final "Next" submitted one near-empty response (Q1 only).
