# Naming LILITH's grown-up sister

Research date: October 19, 2026. Method: brainstorm against the brief, then web search for each shortlisted name: App Store (`site:apps.apple.com <name>`), health and wellness brands, Justia/USPTO trademark records, and domain mentions.

**Caveats, stated up front:**
- Web search is not a clearance search. Justia snapshots can be stale. Before spending money, run the finalists through USPTO Trademark Search (Classes 9, 42, 44), EUIPO, WIPO Global Brand Database, and the UAE Ministry of Economy register.
- **Domains are UNVERIFIED for every name.** No search returned a live site on any exact `.com`. Short real words and given names almost always have a registered (often parked) `.com`. Plan on `get<name>.com`, `<name>.app`, or `<name>health.com`, and check with a registrar.

## The brief, condensed into filters
1. It sounds like a fashion house or a fragrance: two or three syllables, one clear stress, looks good in caps on a black box.
2. Ideally a double meaning across **body + self + sky**.
3. It's a sister to LILITH: mythic, female, a bit dangerous, adult. Never cute.
4. Banned territory: moon/luna alone, aura, glow, bloom, flo, thrive, her-, "girl" puns, generic wellness words.
5. Easy to say in English. Arabic, Greek and Latin roots are welcome.
6. Works for a woman of 22 and a woman of 55. A 55-year-old in perimenopause has to be happy to tell her endocrinologist the name.

## Shortlist of 10

### 1. ZUHRA
- **Meaning and story:** In Arabic, *al-Zuhara* is Venus, the morning and evening star and the brightest thing in the sky after the moon. The root means "to shine, be radiant". Venus is also ♀, the medical symbol for female.
- **Why it fits:** It covers all three meanings in one word. **Body:** ♀, the sign every lab report uses. **Self:** Venus as beauty, desire, confidence, which is the alter ego. **Sky:** a real planet she can find at dusk. It's rooted in the Gulf where the founder lives and still reads as easy and exotic in English. It sits naturally next to LILITH: both are female celestial figures from Near Eastern lore.
- **Pronunciation:** ZOO-ra (two syllables, stress on the first).
- **Risk:** English speakers will drift to ZAH-ra. **Atelier Zuhra** is a Dubai couture label (apparel only, but it's in the founder's home market). The ZAHRA marks owned by Inditex (perfume, Class 3) and Zahra LLC (skincare) are close in sound but in different classes. **Conflicts found:** no App Store app named Zuhra, and no exact US trademark hit. Domain: unverified.

### 2. HESPER
- **Meaning and story:** Greek *Hesperos* is the evening star, again Venus, and the father of the Hesperides, the nymphs who guarded the golden apples at the western edge of the world.
- **Why it fits:** It's an evening and twilight name, so it suits the monthly-rhythm, end-of-day check-in. It's quieter and more adult than Vesper. Two crisp syllables give it a fragrance-house feel (think Hermès, Hesper). Sky and self come through clearly. Body comes through Venus, though less directly than Zuhra.
- **Pronunciation:** HESS-per.
- **Risk:** It's only one letter from the much busier Vesper. A pending US application for **HESPER** (serial 97824784, 2023) covers bath preparations and beverages, and the near-identical Australian **HESPERA** covers skincare. Neither covers software, but both are near the beauty space. **App Store:** only a municipal app (Hesperange, Luxembourg). Domain: unverified.

### 3. COSIMA
- **Meaning and story:** Italian feminine form of Cosmo, from Greek *kosmos*: order, ornament, and the universe. The same root gives us both "cosmetic" (adorning the body) and "cosmos" (ordering the sky).
- **Why it fits:** This is the best literal double meaning on the list. **Body:** cosmetic, adornment. **Sky:** cosmos. **Self:** order out of chaos, which is exactly what the app does for overlapping conditions. It already sounds like a fashion house, and Cosima Wagner gives it real cultural weight.
- **Pronunciation:** KOH-zee-ma (or KOZ-ee-ma).
- **Risk:** The women's-health neighbourhood is crowded with near-names: **Cosia** is a live App Store women's-health app doing cycle, sleep, energy and mood insights (nearly identical positioning), and **Cosma Health** is a doctor-led women's health therapeutics brand. Trademarks: **COSIMA** is registered (Reg. 5921877) for clothing in Class 25, and a new COSIMA application (Buona Stella IP LLC, Aug 2026, class unknown) is pending. It's a common given name, so the `.com` is very likely taken. Domain: unverified.

### 4. NAAMAH
- **Meaning and story:** Hebrew for "pleasant, lovely". In the Zohar, Naamah is Lilith's companion, sometimes described as "another aspect of Lilith". She is one of the four queens of the demons, and the seducer of angels.
- **Why it fits:** It's the most literal "sister of LILITH" anyone could pick. Insiders get the reference and everyone else just hears a pretty word. The palindrome-like shape looks good as a logo.
- **Pronunciation:** NAH-ah-mah (three syllables). People will guess "NAY-ma" and "NAH-ma".
- **Risk:** The double-A spelling trips people up. "Mother of demons" lore is a liability for a health product used with doctors and in Gulf markets. It's also a liability with the strategy of growing up from LILITH. **Conflicts found:** the only US trademark is a cancelled Class 3 skin-lotion mark. The only App Store app with the name is a Hindu baby-names reference. The name is clean, but the brand risk is real. Domain: unverified.

### 5. SIDERA
- **Meaning and story:** Latin for "the stars". *Desiderare* (to desire, to long for, literally "away from the stars") and *considerare* (to consider) both come from it.
- **Why it fits:** It pairs desire (self, the alter ego) with stars (sky), and "consider" is what the doctor report asks her clinician to do. It's elegant and Latin, and sounds like an Italian maison.
- **Pronunciation:** SID-er-a (stress on the first syllable). Some will say si-DEER-a.
- **Risk:** **Sidera: Guidance** is a live App Store app (Sidera LLC, updated Sept 2026, "personal growth and self-reflection" with experts). That's an adjacent category, and a real collision in search results and trademark Class 9/45. The AU trademark **SIDERA** covers awnings (irrelevant). Pharmanutra's SiderAL iron supplement is near in sound and in health. A 2017 crypto project also used the name. Domain: unverified, and likely held.

### 6. METIS
- **Meaning and story:** The Greek Titaness of *metis*, cunning practical intelligence. Zeus swallowed her and she went on counselling him from the inside, and she is Athena's mother.
- **Why it fits:** It's an "inner wisdom" story, a smart-woman name that fits the doctor-ready report and the pattern-finding.
- **Pronunciation:** MEE-tiss or MAY-tiss.
- **Risk:** It's a **serious cultural collision**: the Métis are a recognised Indigenous people in Canada, and the name will read as appropriation. Axelera AI holds **METIS** for AI chips (Class 9). It's also crowded in blockchain and AI. Weak on body and sky. Not recommended.

### 7. ICHOR
- **Meaning and story:** In Greek myth, ichor is the golden fluid in the veins of the gods: divine blood.
- **Why it fits:** It's dark, sexy, adult, and very LILITH-adjacent. Blood plus divinity covers body, self and sky in one hit, and it sounds like a niche perfume.
- **Pronunciation:** EYE-kor.
- **Risk:** **In medicine, "ichor" means a thin, foul discharge from a wound or ulcer.** Any clinician who reads her doctor report will know that. Ichor Holdings (NASDAQ: ICHR, semiconductors) holds **ICHOR** marks, and iCHOR is a vascular-clot medical device company. No App Store collision. Disqualified for a health product on the medical meaning alone.

### 8. APOGEE
- **Meaning and story:** The farthest point of an orbit from Earth. Black Moon Lilith *is* the lunar apogee (the founder's ChartEngine literally computes it).
- **Why it fits:** It's the cleverest sister story: LILITH was the point, APOGEE is her at her highest. It means "peak", which works for confidence.
- **Pronunciation:** AP-uh-jee.
- **Risk:** It's a common English word, so it's weak as a trademark and generic-feeling. **Apogee Lifestyle LLC** owns APOGEE WELLNESS and APOGEE FITNESS & WELLNESS (skincare, supplements, apparel), and APOGEE NEVER PEAK covers fitness and nutrition education. Apogee is also a well-known audio brand. It reads as tech or aerospace, not lux.

### 9. VESPER
- **Meaning and story:** The Latin evening star, and the evening prayer. Also the Bond girl and the cocktail.
- **Why it fits:** Sexy, adult, fragrance-like. It would have ranked near the top.
- **Pronunciation:** VES-per.
- **Risk:** **This is a direct collision.** A live App Store app called **Vesper** is a women's health companion (cycle awareness, iron, hydration). There's also **Vesper AI** (a life coach), Vesperly, and the old Q Branch notes app, plus heavy use in spirits and scooters. **Out.**

### 10. THEIA
- **Meaning and story:** The Titaness of sight and shining light, and mother of the sun, moon and dawn.
- **Why it fits:** "She who sees" fits the receipts-and-patterns promise.
- **Pronunciation:** THAY-a.
- **Risk:** **This is a direct collision.** **Theia Health** is a live app that reads Apple Health, Oura, Fitbit and Garmin with glucose sensors, which is the exact same data layer. There's also a French medication-reading app called Theia. **Out.**

## Top 5, ranked

1. **ZUHRA** — Venus in Arabic, ♀ on a lab report, and the brightest planet. It's the cleanest three-way double meaning, rooted where the founder lives, and nothing obvious is in its lane. Honest caveats: people will say "Zahra", and Atelier Zuhra in Dubai means she should check UAE registration first.
2. **HESPER** — The evening star, the adult version of Vesper without Vesper's collisions. It's quiet, lux, and fragrance-coded. Honest caveats: it's less instantly meaningful, body only comes through Venus, and a pending HESPER bath-products application is nearby.
3. **COSIMA** — The best story on the list (cosmetic + cosmos + order from chaos) and the most fashion-house sound. Honest caveat: the near-names Cosia (a women's-health app with the same positioning) and Cosma Health make it the riskiest of the three on confusion. Ranked third for that reason, not for quality.
4. **SIDERA** — Stars plus desire is gorgeous Latin. Honest caveat: a live "Sidera: Guidance" self-reflection app is too close for comfort. She'd need a modifier or a buyout.
5. **NAAMAH** — The perfect insider sister to LILITH, and clean on trademarks. Honest caveat: the spelling and pronunciation cost her, and the "mother of demons" lore works against a product doctors are meant to trust.

**Dropped after the checks:** Vesper and Theia (live, direct women's-health and wearable-health conflicts), Ichor (its medical meaning is a wound discharge), Metis (Indigenous identity collision), and Apogee (generic, and an existing wellness trademark family).

**Next steps before committing:**
- Get a professional knockout search on Zuhra and Hesper in the US, EU and UAE, Classes 9, 42 and 44.
- Check the domains at a registrar.
- Test pronunciation of Zuhra with 10 English speakers.
- Mock each finalist as a black-box wordmark next to LILITH.
